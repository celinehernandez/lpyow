Welcome to LPYOW

Ready. Set. Lead.

LPYOW is a campus website designed for the students and community of Lyceum of the Philippines University–Cavite. It serves as a centralized platform for accessing campus announcements, upcoming events, student activities, academic resources, and other essential university information. Its goal is to make campus life more connected, accessible, and convenient through technology.

About us

This is who we are

A connected and thriving digital campus, LPYOW (Lyceum Portal: Your Online World) was created to bring the Lyceum of the Philippines University–Cavite community closer together. Through technology, we make campus information more accessible and student life more connected.

We believe that access to information plays an important role in every student’s university journey. LPYOW provides a convenient space where students can discover campus announcements, upcoming events, student activities, and essential university information.

Our Vision

To become a reliable and accessible digital platform that enhances campus life at LPU Cavite through innovation, connection, and technology. We envision a university community where every student can stay informed, engaged, and connected.
